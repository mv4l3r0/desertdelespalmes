A 14 km loop from the monastery to the summit of the Bartolo, past springs, hermitages and the old convent.

At a glance. A loop of about 14 km with 735 m of ascent that starts at the Desert de les Palmes monastery, climbs to the summit of the Bartolo and returns past springs, hermitages and the ruins of the Convent Vell. It takes about 5 hours and is the most complete route for getting to know the heart of the Desert.

The route

The start is next to the monastery. The route first follows the paved road and, after about 530 metres, a path leaves it through the Coscollar del Desert and rejoins the road, now sharing its route with the PR-CV 122, near the Bartolo cross. From there it climbs to the summit, where the hermitage of Sant Miquel stands.

The descent then follows the forest track of the Camí Nou del Bartolo, which passes the Alt del Colomer, the Pla dels Bous and the Font de Roc. It then takes the Font Tallada path, a stretch shared with the PR-CV 397, and further down a path leads to the hermitage and spring of Sant Josep, passing through the Pla de Montserrat.

After crossing the CV-147 road, the route links tracks and paths to the Sant Joan and Sant Elies hermit caves (the «antros») and the spring and hermitage of Naixement. After the Corral de Sant Elies it reaches the Font de la Teula, climbs the Desert ravine past the Convent Vell and the Font del Cristo, and ends along the road, about a hundred metres from the starting point.

Getting to the start

The start is at the Desert de les Palmes monastery, on the CV-147 road (Benicàssim). There is parking at the Old Monastery viewpoint (CV-147, km 9). See the home page for more access details.

Tips and safety

  • Carry enough water: there are springs on the route, but they do not always flow.
  • Check fire risk and access restrictions before setting out.
  • Signage may be out of date: carry the track and a map, and do not rely only on your phone.
  • Respect the hermitages, the hermit caves and the vegetation: do not pick plants or light fires.
  • Suitable footwear and clothing for the season.
