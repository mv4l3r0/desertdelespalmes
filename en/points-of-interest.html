Points of interest

What to see and where to get information in and around the Desert de les Palmes.

«La Bartola» Information Centre

The park's official information centre, on the Desert road (CV-147), km 8, in Benicàssim. Opening times and activities change: check the park's official website.

Monastery and spirituality

Activities and programme are run by the association of friends of the monastery. Visit their website. Benicàssim town council also points to a museum of the Carmelite fathers next to the convent church, with sacred art and old liqueur machinery.

Magdalena hermitage and Castell Vell

Two historic places around the park, in the municipality of Castelló de la Plana, listed by Benicàssim town council among the points of interest on its park routes. Description to be expanded with sources.

Desierto de las Palmas restaurant

A restaurant on a spur near the Carmelite monastery, with views of the Mediterranean. According to the Repsol Guide, it has served lunches and rice dishes since 1967. Check opening times and bookings yourself, as they change.
