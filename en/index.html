Desert de les Palmes

Where the mountain looks out to sea

Where it is and how to get there

The Desert de les Palmes Natural Park covers the range above the Benicàssim coast, in the Plana Alta region (Castellón).

Boundaries

It spans five municipalities: Benicàssim, Cabanes, La Pobla Tornesa, Borriol and Castelló de la Plana. It covers about 3,200 hectares and its highest point is the Bartolo, at 729 metres. It was declared a Natural Area in October 1989.

By car

From Benicàssim, take the local road CV-147. From Borriol, La Pobla Tornesa or Cabanes, take the CV-10 dual carriageway and then the CV-148. On the AP-7, leave at exit 46 (Castellón Norte–Benicàssim).

Information and parking

The La Bartola Information Centre is on the CV-147 at km 8 (Benicàssim). There is parking at the San José viewpoint and at the Old Monastery viewpoint (CV-147, km 9).
